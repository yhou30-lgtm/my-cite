I'm Jack. This site is where I write about what I am learning and what I can't stop thinking about, so far a poem, an integral and a question about what makes a life good.

I'm a university student, and I use writing to check whether I understand something. If I can explain it plainly, I keep it. If I can't, I go back to the reading.

Everything here is my own work and my own reading, so I may be wrong. If you spot a mistake or disagree with something, I would like to hear it.
